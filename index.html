ABOUT DR. ANANYA

Listening first. Understanding deeply.

Dr. Ananya Nair is a Consultant Dermatologist based in Kochi, Kerala. Her practice combines evidence-based dermatological care with a thoughtful understanding of each patient's individual concerns.

Her clinical interests include common and complex skin conditions, acne and acne scarring, pigmentation disorders, hair and scalp concerns, and aesthetic dermatology.
